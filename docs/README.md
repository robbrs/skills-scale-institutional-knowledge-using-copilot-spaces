## Process at a Glance

OctoAcme follows an iterative lifecycle: initiate and validate work, plan delivery, execute and track progress, release, then learn and improve. Project Managers coordinate delivery, Product Managers define outcomes and priorities, and the team delivers tested increments with stakeholder input.

- **Initiation:** Confirm the need and success measures, identify stakeholders, prepare a lightweight project brief and initial risks, and decide whether to proceed.
- **Planning:** Prioritize and estimate backlog items, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, and agree on milestones, dependencies, and a QA approach.
- **Execution and tracking:** Manage work on a project board, use small reviewed changes and automated testing, and maintain alignment through standups, delivery syncs, and demos.
- **Risk and communication:** Maintain and regularly review a risk register, share status with stakeholders, and escalate blockers through the agreed path.
- **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria and CI/security checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, deploy, verify, and notify stakeholders.
- **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** After sprints, releases, or key milestones, capture learnings, assign and track action items, and feed improvements back into the work.
