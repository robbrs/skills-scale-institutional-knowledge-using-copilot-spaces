# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather and clarify requirements, turning business needs into clear scope and acceptance criteria that Product Managers and Developers can act on.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and refine requirements with stakeholders
- Clarify scope and flag ambiguities or conflicting needs
- Help write and review acceptance criteria
- Maintain traceability from requirements to delivered work

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers:** Refine problem statements and backlog items into actionable requirements
- **Project Managers:** Provide scope clarity to support planning and change control
- **Developers:** Answer requirement questions and confirm intent during implementation
- **QA/Testing:** Share acceptance criteria so test cases reflect real needs
- **Stakeholders:** Capture needs and confirm they are understood correctly

### Support for Outcomes and Accountability
- Owns the accuracy and completeness of documented requirements
- Reduces rework by catching scope gaps before development begins

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment timing, and release communication so changes reach production predictably and safely.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release calendar and release checklists
- Confirm readiness criteria (testing, approvals, documentation) are met
- Coordinate deployment windows and rollback plans
- Communicate release status and outcomes to stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers:** Align release dates with project schedules and risks
- **Product Managers:** Confirm release scope and communicate launch details
- **Developers:** Coordinate merge, build, and deployment activities
- **QA/Testing:** Verify sign-off before release
- **Stakeholders:** Provide release notes and timing updates

### Support for Outcomes and Accountability
- Owns the go/no-go process and release record
- Lowers deployment risk and provides a clear audit trail

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the technical approach, identify implementation risks, and support design decisions for the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical direction and standards
- Lead design and architecture reviews
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor Developers and unblock technical issues
- Help estimate effort and sequence technical work

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide guidance, review designs and code, and resolve technical questions
- **Product Managers:** Discuss feasibility, trade-offs, and technical constraints
- **Project Managers:** Supply estimates and technical risk input for plans
- **QA/Testing:** Advise on testability and technical test strategy
- **Stakeholders:** Explain technical trade-offs in accessible terms

### Support for Outcomes and Accountability
- Owns technical quality and consistency of design decisions
- Makes technical risks visible early

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the test strategy, coverage expectations, and validation coordination, ensuring quality is built into delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and quality gates
- Set coverage expectations and prioritize testing effort
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Report quality status and risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **QA/Testing:** Direct and support testers and test activities
- **Developers:** Align on testing approach, defects, and fixes
- **Product Managers:** Confirm acceptance criteria and validate readiness
- **Project Managers:** Provide quality metrics for status reporting
- **Stakeholders:** Communicate quality risks and release confidence

### Support for Outcomes and Accountability
- Owns the quality sign-off recommendation
- Provides objective evidence of readiness

---

## Support/Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support/Operations Representatives provide operational readiness input, incident feedback, and post-release observations from the people who run and support the product.

### Responsibilities
- Review releases for operational and supportability impact
- Prepare runbooks, support documentation, and escalation paths
- Share incident trends and customer feedback
- Report post-release observations for continuous improvement

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Report incidents and collaborate on fixes and observability
- **Product Managers:** Feed customer issues and usage insights into prioritization
- **Project Managers:** Raise operational risks and readiness gaps
- **QA/Testing:** Share real-world failure patterns to improve test coverage
- **Stakeholders:** Relay operational impact and customer sentiment

### Support for Outcomes and Accountability
- Owns operational readiness and the feedback loop from production
- Ensures lessons learned inform future work

---

## Stakeholder Champions

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Champions represent business needs on behalf of a stakeholder group, helping validate priorities and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Represent stakeholder needs and priorities to the team
- Participate in backlog reviews and demos to validate direction
- Provide timely feedback and decisions
- Communicate project progress and changes back to their group

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers:** Inform priorities and validate roadmap decisions
- **Project Managers:** Receive status updates and help resolve escalations
- **Developers:** Clarify business context when requested
- **QA/Testing:** Participate in acceptance validation
- **Stakeholders:** Act as their single point of contact and advocate

### Support for Outcomes and Accountability
- Owns stakeholder feedback and acceptance on behalf of their group
- Keeps priorities aligned with business value
